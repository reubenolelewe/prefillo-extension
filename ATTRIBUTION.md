# Upstream attribution

Prefillo is forked from [Little AI Helper / job-autofill-extension](https://github.com/ritsth/job-autofill-extension) by Ritika Shrestha and contributors, pinned at commit `fb19fcd4e084c19b40a2207bda0c21ab09cddbf0` (October 9, 2026). The upstream MIT notice is retained in `LICENSE`.

Prefillo actively reuses and adapts the Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, and Workday page-info adapters in `src/content/adapters/`, the shared label/value setter in `src/content/adapters/shared.ts`, and the host matching helper in `src/lib/host.ts`. The extension manifest, license gate, contact-only fill path, and Prefillo side panel are new Prefillo code.
